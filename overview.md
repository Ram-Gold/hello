# Hello — Application Overview

**Hello** is a fast, private personal relationship keeper for Android. It helps you remember the people you meet, capture quick notes about your interactions, and stay on top of follow-ups—without relying on the cloud or sharing your data.

---

## Core Purpose
* **Remember people effortlessly:** Keep a dedicated space for the people in your life, separate from your phone's cluttered address book.
* **Capture encounters in seconds:** Open the app and start typing immediately—designed to launch and bring up the keyboard almost instantaneously so you never lose a thought after meeting someone.
* **Stay connected:** Track upcoming reminders and recent encounters so important follow-ups don't slip through the cracks.

---

## Key Principles

### 1. 100% Private & Offline
* **No accounts, no cloud, no tracking:** Everything you write stays strictly on your phone. There are no servers, no sign-ups, and no background data collection.
* **Respectful permissions:**
  * **Your phone contacts stay untouched:** Hello never asks to read or modify your device's contacts list.
  * **Location only when you're in the moment:** If you choose to record where you met someone, Hello only checks your location while you are actively logging the encounter—never in the background. Denying location access never stops you from using the app.

### 2. You Own Your Data
* **Easy backups & portability:** You can export your entire history at any time into a bundle of standard, human-readable files (including plain-text notes and spreadsheets) and save them wherever you like on your device.

### 3. Calm, Editorial Design
* **Minimalist look & feel:** Built with a clean, paper-like aesthetic in both light and dark modes, using subtle borders, rounded cards, and readable typography (**Mallow** for headings and **Vellum** for body text) so your notes are pleasant to read and write.
* **Quick access:** Includes home screen widgets and a dedicated quick-add experience so logging a new person or memory takes minimal effort.
